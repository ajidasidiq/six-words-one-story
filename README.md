An entry for the make.fun bounty **Six Words, One Story**: a complete story told in exactly six words.

## The story

> The lighthouse keeper lit nothing tonight.

A keeper's one duty is the light. Tonight he lit nothing — and the story is everything that sentence does not say: why he stopped, who was waiting for the beam, and what happens to the ships out in the dark.

## What it does

`main.ts` writes the six-word story to `/output/entry.txt`, after checking it is exactly six words and within the 60-character gate.

## How to run

Requires Node 22 or later:

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
